About Me

Hi, I'm Chris Lovejoy 👋 I build AI products for complex, domain-specific verticals like healthcare and life sciences.

Currently: Member of Technical Staff at Anthropic.

Previously: founding team at Anterior (healthcare LLM startup backed by Sequoia/NEA), medical doctor, academic researcher, and ML engineer. (I describe that journey here).

I write about building vertical AI products that generate real-world value. I previously wrote a book about learning medicine and lectured on AI for healthcare.
